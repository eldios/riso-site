Open-source Linux ricing framework

One palette, your whole desktop.

A theme is data. riso renders its palette, wallpapers and hand-written files into the configs your terminals, bars, notifiers, lockers and editors read. Seventeen applications work out of the box.

How it works

Theme data becomes desktop configuration.

riso keeps the theme, generated fragments and your own configuration separate.

A theme is data

A theme contains a palette, wallpapers and hand-written files.

riso renders it

riso turns that data into config fragments for seventeen applications out of the box.

Your configs include it

Your configs stay yours. They include riso's fragments, and riso restore puts back every byte.

Works with your Linux desktop.

riso works on Hyprland, Sway, niri and Omarchy. It reads Omarchy themes byte-for-byte compatibly.

Install: Arch family from the AUR with riso or riso-bin, NixOS via flake, and deb or rpm from releases.

Hyprland Sway niri Omarchy Arch NixOS deb rpm
